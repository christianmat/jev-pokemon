What is Jev?

A model that decides instead of writing

Jev from TypeSafe AI doesn't generate text. You give it a situation and a list of options, and it returns a choice with a probability for each one. We wanted to see how far that gets you in a game with hundreds of decisions, so we pointed it at Pokémon Red.

01Read the game's memory: map, party, battle, on-screen text.
02List the legal options, each with facts like type matchups and distance to the goal.
03Ask Jev to choose, then press the buttons to carry out its choice.
